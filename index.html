THE RULES

Get close. Don't cross.

  1. Choose your decade.Every season in your eight-round run comes from the era you select.
  2. Read the assignment.Each round gives you an NBA season and a totals category.
  3. Choose one player.Pick the player you think finished closest to 50th in that category.
  4. Stay inside the line.Ranks 1–50 score their rank. Rank 51+ scores zero.
  5. Try Triple Take.Use three revealed player ranks to total as close to 100 as possible without crossing it.
  6. Or beat the clock.Shot Clock mode gives you 24 seconds per pick. Let it hit zero and it's an automatic miss.
